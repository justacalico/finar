# 3.0.0

- Removes Flutter
- Port the entire app to Tauri, Rust, and Web.
- Support for resizing
- Native video rendering
- Heavy performance increase.
- Downloads: movies, series (download all), seasons (download season), episodes; metadata and organized paths (Show/Season 01/, Movies/).
- Music library: Albums, Artists, Tracks tabs; AlbumCard component; album detail with track list; no song views (redirect to album); detail art for albums and artists.
