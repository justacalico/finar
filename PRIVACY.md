# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** February 2026

## Summary

**Finar does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage data.**

## Data collection

We do **not** collect:

- Personal information (name, email, etc.)
- Usage or analytics data
- Device identifiers
- Location data
- Any other data about you or how you use the app

## Your data stays on your devices

- App settings and preferences are stored **only on your device** (e.g. via local storage such as shared preferences).
- All media streaming is between **your device and your own Jellyfin server**. We do not have access to your server, your library, or what you watch.
- We do not run any tracking, analytics, or third‑party services that gather data from the app.

## Changes

If this policy is updated, the change will be reflected in this document. Continued use of Finar after updates means you accept the revised policy.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue or contact the project maintainers via the [GitLab repository](https://gitlab.com/Openlyst/finar).
